# Photographing your work for the website

A guide for ArtbyMoon. The website shows each piece at its true shape, never cropped, so the photographs you take are exactly what visitors will see. Good photos matter more than anything else on the site.

You don't need a professional camera. A recent phone works well if you follow the steps below.

## The basics for every piece

- **Light:** daylight from a large window, on a bright but overcast day, or with the sun not shining directly on the work. Turn room lights off: mixing daylight and bulbs makes colours go yellow or blue. Avoid flash.
- **No glare:** acrylic and varnish reflect. If you see a shiny patch, move the work or yourself a little until it's gone. Shooting slightly from one side and then straightening the photo later is not ideal, so it's better to move the light.
- **Straight on:** hold the camera exactly parallel to the work, centred, so the edges come out as straight lines and not a trapezium. Using a tripod or resting the phone on a stack of books helps a lot. On a phone, turn on the grid lines.
- **Fill the frame:** get close enough that the work fills most of the photo. Don't zoom digitally: step closer instead, or use the phone's 2× lens if it has one (it distorts less).
- **Colour check:** compare the photo with the real piece side by side. If colours are off, adjust only white balance and brightness. Don't add filters.
- **Keep the original:** keep the full-size photo from the camera. Don't send photos through WhatsApp or Instagram first, because they shrink them.

## What to send for each type of work

| Work                            | Main photo                                                                    | Extra photos (optional, but lovely)                                | Shape                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Acrylic paintings on canvas** | The whole painting, straight on, cropped to the painted edge                  | A close-up of brushwork; the painting on a wall or easel for scale | The canvas's own shape (e.g. 4:5, 3:4, 1:1, 4:3)                                              |
| **Bookmarks**                   | The front, flat, on a plain background                                        | The back (quotes, lettering); one in a book or in a hand for scale | About 1:3 to 1:3.5, tall and slim                                                             |
| **Polaroid-style paintings**    | The whole polaroid **including the white frame and your handwritten caption** | A close-up of the painting area                                    | About 0.82:1 (frame included)                                                                 |
| **Sketches and cards**          | The whole page or card, flat, straight on                                     | A detail; a card standing up                                       | The paper's own shape                                                                         |
| **Photography**                 | Your photograph as you want it seen                                           | —                                                                  | Any shape. On the site, photos may be shown in a grid, but the full frame is always available |

**Background for small pieces** (bookmarks, polaroids, cards): plain white or light grey paper, slightly larger than the piece. Your notebook-and-dried-flowers style is lovely for Instagram, but for the main website photo, keep it plain so the work is the focus. Styled shots are perfect as the optional extra photos.

## Size and format

- **Resolution:** at least **3000 pixels** on the long side (a recent phone photo is usually 4000 or more, so that's fine).
- **Format:** **JPEG** at high quality is perfect. HEIC (the iPhone default) is fine too: send it as it is, and it will be converted to JPEG when it is added to the site. Don't send screenshots.
- **Colour:** standard sRGB (phones do this automatically).
- **Photography only:** please remove the location from your photos before sending, especially photos taken at home. On iPhone, tap Options when sharing and turn off Location.

## Naming the files

Name each file so it's easy to match to the details you send:

```
collection-short-name-view.jpg
```

- **collection:** `canvas`, `abstract`, `polaroid`, `sketch`, `bookmark` or `photo`
- **short-name:** a few words, lowercase, with hyphens. Use the title if the work has one (e.g. `river-at-dusk`), otherwise anything memorable (`blue-flowers-2025`)
- **view:** `main`, `detail`, `back`, `context`

Examples:

- `canvas-river-at-dusk-main.jpg`
- `bookmark-lavender-quote-back.jpg`
- `polaroid-autumn-lake-main.jpg`

The short name becomes part of the web address for the piece, so try not to change it later.

## The details to send with each piece

Only what you want shown. Anything you leave out simply doesn't appear, and nothing is ever guessed.

| Detail                                                    | Example                                     | Needed?                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Description of what's in the picture (for blind visitors) | "A lavender field at dusk under a pink sky" | **Yes**, one sentence                                    |
| Title                                                     | (example) River at Dusk                     | Optional                                                 |
| Year                                                      | (example) 2025                              | Optional                                                 |
| Medium                                                    | Acrylic on canvas                           | Optional                                                 |
| Size                                                      | 40 × 50 cm                                  | Optional                                                 |
| Subject (canvas only)                                     | landscape, portrait, animal, flower         | Optional                                                 |
| Available, sold, not for sale, or commission only         | Sold                                        | Optional. Nothing is shown as for sale unless you say so |
| Polaroid caption                                          | The words you wrote on it, exactly          | Optional                                                 |
| A few words about the piece, in your own voice            | —                                           | Optional                                                 |

A simple spreadsheet with one row per piece is the easiest way to send these.

## Before you send: a quick check

- [ ] The whole piece is in the photo, with straight edges
- [ ] No glare or shadows across the work
- [ ] Colours look like the real thing
- [ ] Full-size original files (not sent through a messaging app)
- [ ] Files named as above, with details in the spreadsheet
- [ ] Location removed from photography
